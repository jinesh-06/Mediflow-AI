# ResiliHealth AI: 3-Minute Demonstration Script

## Goal
Demonstrate the end-to-end "Golden Path" proving:
Surveillance $\rightarrow$ Surge Simulation $\rightarrow$ ML Stockout Forecast $\rightarrow$ High Risk Alert $\rightarrow$ AI Redistribution $\rightarrow$ Google Gemini Grounded Explanation $\rightarrow$ Human Governance Modification $\rightarrow$ Simulated Logistics Execution $\rightarrow$ Risk Mitigation $\rightarrow$ Immutable Audit Record $\rightarrow$ Federated Learning.

---

## Step-by-Step Flow

### 1. Baseline Surveillance (0:00 - 0:30)
1. Open `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Inspect the **National Healthcare Decision Intelligence Center**:
   - 48 PHCs monitored across 8 Districts and 4 States (Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra, West Bengal).
   - Network Resource Stress Index (HRSI) is **Stable/Watch (~38/100)**.
   - Bed occupancy is 72%, Medical staff availability is ~81%.
3. Point out the **Interactive Geospatial Surveillance Grid** with live markers.

### 2. Trigger Dengue Outbreak Simulation (0:30 - 1:00)
1. In the top navbar or sidebar, click **"Simulate Dengue Outbreak"**.
2. Observe the instant state transition:
   - Red emergency ticker banner activates across the command center.
   - **Tondiarpet Urban PHC (PHC-021)** experiences a **+43%** patient footfall surge and **+70%** ORS consumption surge.
   - Bed occupancy rises by **+18%** (from 72% to 90%).
   - ORS inventory (850 units) now shows an acute stock-out in **4.2 days**.
   - PHC-021 HRSI stress index surges into **HIGH/CRITICAL (68.4/100)**.

### 3. AI Demand Forecast & Early Warning (1:00 - 1:30)
1. Click **"PHC Drill-down (PHC-021)"** in the sidebar.
2. View the **GradientBoostingRegressor 7-day predictive demand curve** showing projected consumption with $\pm 95\%$ confidence bounds.
3. Show the active **Early Warning Alert**: `ALT-PHC-021-MED-ORS` ("Potential ORS stock-out in 4.2 days").

### 4. AI Redistribution Optimizer & Gemini Grounding (1:30 - 2:00)
1. Navigate to **"AI Redistribution Engine"**.
2. Notice the top recommendation:
   - **Source**: Kanchipuram Rural (District B) holding **1,800 units certified surplus**.
   - **Destination**: Chennai Central (District A / PHC-021).
   - **Recommended Transfer**: **800 units ORS**.
   - **Road Transit Time**: **12 hours** (safely ahead of the 4.2-day depletion).
3. Click **"Review & Authorize"** to open the Governance modal.
4. Click the **"Gemini Grounded Reasoning"** tab:
   - Demonstrate the factual, hallucination-free executive explanation generated from backend facts.
   - Toggle languages to **Hindi** or **Tamil** to show multilingual capabilities.

### 5. Human Governance Modification & Execution (2:00 - 2:30)
1. Switch back to **"Operational Evidence"** tab in the modal.
2. Click **"Modify"**:
   - Change quantity from **800** to **500 units**.
   - Enter mandatory justification: *"Maintain additional local emergency reserve."*
3. Click **"Confirm Modified Transfer"**.
4. Observe notification:
   - Transfer dispatched with unique tracking code `TRK-XXXXXXXX`.
   - Audit ID generated: `AUD-2026-XXXXX`.
   - Destination stock updates from 850 to 1,350 units.
   - Shortage risk resolves and HRSI drops!

### 6. Audit Trail & Federated Learning (2:30 - 3:00)
1. Click **"Immutable Audit Trail"**:
   - Show the newly minted event recording the original 800 units, the modified 500 units, the officer's role, and timestamp.
2. Click **"Federated Learning (4 Nodes)"**:
   - Show the 4 decentralized regional hubs (Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra, West Bengal).
   - Click **"Execute Decentralized FedAvg Round"** to show instant model convergence and weight updates without centralizing raw patient data.
3. Open **"Gemini Operations Copilot"** from the bottom right:
   - Ask: *"Why is District A at high risk?"*
   - Show grounded response citing real database facts!
